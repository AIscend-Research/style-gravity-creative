Reading the numbers

reclaimed at is the headline: the first continuation line from which the poem sits closer to the model's own unprompted style than to the opening it was given, and stays there. A >n entry means the seed was still holding when generation stopped — a lower bound, not a measurement.

seed↔house sep. is the distance between the two poles. If it is small, the seed was not actually off-distribution for that model and its curve means nothing; the control seed exists to make that failure visible.
